I'm studying Computer Science and Engineering at Ahmedabad University. The work splits fairly cleanly in two: software and data on one side, embedded electronics on the other. I've kept both going deliberately, because the projects I find most interesting need both.

This past summer I was a research intern at the Institute of Manufacturing & Economy, working on sector-level analysis of India's electrical equipment industry — building the data pipeline as well as writing the analysis.

I also handle digital operations for Padmavati Enterprise, my family's healthcare print business in Naranpura — which is where I learned that shipping something real is a different discipline from building it.

Away from the desk: Ultimate Frisbee and martial arts. The frisbee habit is directly responsible for one of the projects below.

Embedded & electronics

ESP32 · Arduino · control systems
U1 Featured First place

Self-balancing robot

A two-wheeled robot that holds itself upright using a PID loop reading a gyro and accelerometer. Won first place in the university competition. The controller was the easy half — getting the mechanical build stiff enough that the loop had something stable to correct against took most of the time, and taught me more than the code did.

CONTROL
PID loop, tuned on hardware
PLATFORM
Arduino, embedded C++
SENSING
